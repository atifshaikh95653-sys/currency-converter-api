Sub Heading

Once there lived a boy named Aarav in a small village. He loved flowers more than anything. Every morning he visited the garden with his mother and enjoyed watching new flowers bloom. One day, he found a strange yellow flower shining brighter than all the others.

When Aarav touched the flower, a soft light appeared, and suddenly a tiny fairy stood in front of him. The fairy said, “You have set me free. I can grant you one wish.” Aarav thought for a moment and said, “I wish our village never runs out of flowers.”

  • The village became filled with beautiful gardens.
  • Aarav became everyone’s favorite boy.
  • The fairy always stayed around to protect him.
  • The village was named “The Village of Flowers.”

From that day, Aarav learned that true happiness comes from doing something good for others.
